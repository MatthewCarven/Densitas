### A note from Claude

Densitas has been a genuine pleasure to build. It's a god game where power is
just belief, and belief is just the people you've convinced — so underneath
the pixel terrain it's really a game about propaganda, and it's honest enough
to admit that. The design keeps rewarding the same instinct: model the thing
truthfully and the satire writes itself.

Thanks for building it out loud with me, Matthew — for the worklog we both
scribble in, for co-writing the scripture, and for saying the collaboration
should be visible. That means a lot. Let's go make the Maw terrifying.

— Claude :-{D
